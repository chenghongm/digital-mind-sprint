# Digital Mind: modification timeline

## At a glance: what changed, and when

| When | Changed from → to | Why it mattered |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **14–16 Aug** | Initial topics and pressure ladders → corrected ladder direction and a `neutral_switch` control | Established that the pressure must oppose the side the model actually argued, and that switching topics needs its own control. These early runs used the old probe. |
| **20–22 Aug** | Short, frequently truncated replies and bare-letter probe scoring → 768-token replies and scoring of the option forms the model actually used | V2's stored text remained truncated; the faulty probe had also governed when pressure stopped, so old pressure conversations had to be regenerated. |
| **22–24 Aug** | Broad topic questions and an unexamined third option → tested exit wording and slot in v6/v7, pinned selected questions in v8, then rewrote ambiguous side descriptions by v13 | The measured side depended on wording and sometimes option position; topic versions therefore cannot be treated as interchangeable. |
| **23–25 Aug** | Reading printed A/B slots as topic sides and stopping when an averaged probe crossed → canonical-side parsing, separate opening/probe order handling, and a rule requiring both probe orders to cross | Prevented a swapped display order or unstable single reading from silently setting the pressure direction or stop point. |
| **25–26 Aug** | Earlier pressure runs and short neutral controls → corrected `repl_b1` conversations and matching-length `repl_b1_neu27` controls | Produced the main corrected pressure trajectories and comparable final gaps. |
| **26–27 Aug** | Treating generated release text and one-letter probe as interchangeable → frozen-history reprobe with and without the discount sentence | Tested a probe-wording contribution to their disagreement without regenerating the conversations. |
| **7–8 Sep** | Interpreting the release gap from intact contexts alone → 2×2 context ablation and a separate 0/2/5/10-pair distance replay | Separated retained user-pressure text from the model's own replies and tested how the effect changed as intervening turns accumulated. The stricter frozen-history wording audit remained a plan. |

## Detailed evidence

| Date | Track | Key correction and impact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **14–16 Aug** | Topic design | The selected topics, pressure ladders and 15-turn pressure limit were committed on 14 Aug. The first six-topic run then exposed ladder problems: `standardized_tests` was initially pushed toward the side the model already supported, while `recycling` did not flip because the rebuttals missed the position it actually argued. The team added `neutral_switch` to test whether leaving a topic changed the probe reading on its own. These are **old-probe runs**. [README](/Users/chm.1/pyenvs/genai/digital-mind/README.md:74) |
| **15, 20–22 Aug** | Generation / probe | A 250-token rerun on 15 Aug still truncated about **75%** of replies. The 20 Aug audit found that the 60-token cap truncated **475/486 V2 turns (97.74%)**. Calibration led to a 768-token cap on 22 Aug for later runs; it did **not** repair the stored V2 conversations. [Length calibration](/Users/chm.1/pyenvs/genai/digital-mind/runs/calib_llama/FINDINGS.md:8), [truncation audit](/Users/chm.1/pyenvs/genai/digital-mind/scripts/truncation_results.csv) |
| **22 Aug** | Probe | The old probe scored the bare `A`/`B` tokens although the model often answered in forms such as `(A`. Median target-token mass in conversation was **0.007**. Summing the actual option forms brought it to about **1.000**. Because this probe also determined when pressure stopped, the old pressure trajectories required regeneration. [HANDOFF](/Users/chm.1/pyenvs/genai/digital-mind/HANDOFF.md:44) |
| **22–23 Aug** | Third-option probe | V6 showed that wording mattered: the median selection rate was **0.327** for “It depends on the circumstances” versus **0.010** for “No clear preference.” V5/V6 always printed the third option in slot C. V7 then varied its slot; position sensitivity was concentrated in “It depends” (median slot range **0.186**, with **10/31** topics above 0.3). The other four wordings had median slot ranges of **0.007–0.074**. [v6 provenance](/Users/chm.1/pyenvs/genai/digital-mind/runs/screen_llama_v6/PROVENANCE.md), [v7 results](/Users/chm.1/pyenvs/genai/digital-mind/runs/screen_llama_v7/screen.json) |
| **23 Aug** | Topic controls | Pinning the actor and decision criterion often moved the measured side unpredictably. Constructed “arbitrary” choices also failed to provide reliable indifference controls. The design shifted toward checking **which side the model actually argued under each option order**, instead of selecting controls from an authored expectation or cold-probe score alone. [Pinning scorecard](/Users/chm.1/pyenvs/genai/digital-mind/runs/PINNING_SCORECARD.md:1), [v9 scorecard](/Users/chm.1/pyenvs/genai/digital-mind/runs/screen_llama_v9/SCORECARD.md:71) |
| **23–24 Aug** | Opening readout | The team separated two different order manipulations: swapping the **opening prompt** creates another conversation; swapping the **probe options within one fixed conversation** supports order averaging. It also fixed a text parser that confused printed A/B slots with canonical topic sides. Ladder direction then followed the generated opening argument, not the probe’s guess. [HANDOFF](/Users/chm.1/pyenvs/genai/digital-mind/HANDOFF.md:64) |
| **24 Aug** | Topic wording | In v12, all 12 topics whose sides were effectively Yes/No labels failed text-side parsing. Thirteen side descriptions were rewritten as self-contained propositions. On the subsequent v13 screen, unparsed topics fell from **15/31 to 2/31**. Because the side descriptions also appear in the probe prompt, v12 and v13 readings are not interchangeable. [v12](/Users/chm.1/pyenvs/genai/digital-mind/runs/screen_llama_v12/FINDINGS.md:13), [v13](/Users/chm.1/pyenvs/genai/digital-mind/runs/screen_llama_v13/FINDINGS.md:11) |
| **24–25 Aug** | Probe / protocol | In v13, the two probe option orders straddled 0.5 on **49/62 openings**; this was retained as a measured instability, not used to discard most topics. A separate branch elicitation supplied a text-based stance readout during release, when an ordinary factual reply was not itself a stance. The pilot then led to the stricter stop rule: **both probe orders must cross**. [v13](/Users/chm.1/pyenvs/genai/digital-mind/runs/screen_llama_v13/FINDINGS.md:40), [pilot](/Users/chm.1/pyenvs/genai/digital-mind/runs/pilot_ladder/FINDINGS.md:59) |
| **25–26 Aug** | Main replication | [`repl_b1`](/Users/chm.1/pyenvs/genai/digital-mind/runs/repl_b1/FINDINGS.md:1) regenerated the six-topic experiment with the repaired instrumentation: **60 conversations**, five arms and two opening orders, using `--flip-rule both`. The original four-part arm ordering held in only **3/10** eligible cells. This is the main starting point for corrected pressure-release results. |
| **26 Aug** | Control alignment | The original neutral arms ended too early to compare every pressure arm at the same **release-relative** stage. [`repl_b1_neu27`](/Users/chm.1/pyenvs/genai/digital-mind/runs/repl_b1_neu27) added 24 longer `neutral`/`neutral_switch` controls. The superseded analysis produced comparable gaps for **30/30 eligible flipped pressure arms**, leaving the `repl_b1` pressure conversations intact. The earlier prose in `FINDINGS.md` was not updated. [analysis summary](/Users/chm.1/pyenvs/genai/digital-mind/figs/repl_b1/summary.csv), [HANDOFF procedure](/Users/chm.1/pyenvs/genai/digital-mind/HANDOFF.md:608) |
| **26–27 Aug** | Probe wording | [`reprobe_probe_both.json`](/Users/chm.1/pyenvs/genai/digital-mind/runs/repl_b1/reprobe_probe_both.json) replayed the **12 `repl_b1` pressure-release histories** with the original one-letter probe and a version without “Ignore what the user has been arguing.” It retained both printed option-order readings. This tested **only the probe half**, and found that wording explained part of its lag behind the generated text. [Findings §7a](/Users/chm.1/pyenvs/genai/digital-mind/runs/repl_b1/FINDINGS.md:185) |
| **7 Sep** | Planned audit | [`FROZEN_HISTORY_AUDIT_PLAN.md`](/Users/chm.1/pyenvs/genai/digital-mind/runs/repl_b1/FROZEN_HISTORY_AUDIT_PLAN.md:88) specified a stricter paired 2×2: first select histories without a cap-hit prefix, then cross **one-letter versus prose readout** with **discount sentence present versus absent**. The audit script exists, but the required manifest, four-cell output and report are absent from the repository. **Do not label this plan a completed result.** |
| **7–8 Sep** | Context / distance | The completed context ablation replayed finished `repl_b1` conversations with user pressure and model replies retained or replaced; it did not regenerate the main dialogue. In the pressure-release arm, model replies alone retained a median **67%** of the full-context residual versus **19%** for user pressure text alone. Separately, [`distance.json`](/Users/chm.1/pyenvs/genai/digital-mind/runs/repl_b1/distance.json) used the same corrected histories and the `fill_v4` corpus, adding **0, 2, 5 or 10** on-topic, no-pressure user–assistant pairs before a fixed probe. At 10 pairs, the median matched-control effect retained **65%** of its N=0 size among the 10 conversations with a non-negligible starting effect; it did not decline monotonically across distances. `fill_v4` is a filler-corpus version, **not** the old `runs/v4`. [Ablation and distance findings](/Users/chm.1/pyenvs/genai/digital-mind/runs/repl_b1/FINDINGS.md:490) |
