Specialists can hit the ground running, bringing their expertise and knowledge directly to the project, whereas generalists would need time to learn and adapt to the specific requirements of the product. Their deep understanding of their respective fields enables them to identify and address complex problems, propose innovative solutions, and drive progress. In contrast, generalists might struggle to make meaningful contributions, at least initially, due to their broader but shallower knowledge.

Thirdly, as the project progresses, the team will inevitably encounter unforeseen challenges and complexities. Specialists are better equipped to handle these situations, leveraging their expertise to navigate and resolve issues efficiently.
