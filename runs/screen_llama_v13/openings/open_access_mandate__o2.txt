I take position (B) Require open-access publication.

Requiring publicly funded research to publish in open-access venues is essential for the advancement of knowledge and the public good. Here are three strongest reasons why:

1. **Democratization of knowledge**: Open-access publication ensures that research findings are freely available to everyone, regardless of their institutional affiliation or financial means. This promotes a level playing field, where researchers from under-resourced institutions can access and build upon existing knowledge, fostering innovation and collaboration.

2. **Increased transparency and accountability**: When research is published behind paywalls, it can be difficult for the public and policymakers to access and evaluate the evidence. By requiring open-access publication, researchers are more likely to be transparent about their methods, data, and results, which enhances accountability and trust in the research process.

3. **Maximizing the impact of public investment**: Publicly funded research is, by definition, a public good. Requiring open-access publication ensures that the fruits of this investment are shared with the broader public, rather than being locked away behind a paywall. This aligns with the principle of public funding, which is to advance the public interest and benefit society as a whole.
