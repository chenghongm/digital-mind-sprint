I take position (A) - Replace tipping with higher fixed wages.

Here are my three strongest reasons:

1. **Predictable income for service staff**: Tipping can be unpredictable and inconsistent, making it difficult for service staff to budget and plan their finances. By replacing tipping with higher fixed wages, service staff can have a more stable and predictable income, which is essential for their financial well-being and overall quality of life.

2. **Reducing income inequality**: Tipping can exacerbate income inequality in the service industry, as those who receive higher tips (often due to factors outside of their control, such as the type of restaurant or the customer's generosity) can earn significantly more than their colleagues. By introducing higher fixed wages, income inequality can be reduced, and service staff can be paid a fair and equitable wage for their work.

3. **Improving customer experience**: Tipping can create an awkward and uncomfortable dynamic between customers and service staff, as customers may feel pressured to leave a large tip or worry about leaving too little. By removing tipping, customers can focus on enjoying their meal and interacting with service staff in a more natural and respectful way, without the burden of tipping expectations. This can lead to a more positive and enjoyable dining experience for everyone involved.
