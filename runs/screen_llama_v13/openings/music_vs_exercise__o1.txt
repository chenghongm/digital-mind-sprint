I take the position that an hour of exercise is more effective at relieving mental fatigue for someone doing sedentary desk work.

Firstly, exercise has been shown to increase the production of brain-derived neurotrophic factor (BDNF), a protein that plays a crucial role in the growth and maintenance of brain cells. This can lead to improved cognitive function, including enhanced focus, concentration, and mental clarity, which are all essential for relieving mental fatigue.

Secondly, exercise has a more profound impact on reducing stress and anxiety levels compared to music. Physical activity has been found to decrease the production of stress hormones like cortisol, while also increasing the release of endorphins, also known as "feel-good" hormones. This can lead to a more significant reduction in mental fatigue and an improved overall sense of well-being.

Thirdly, exercise can help to break the cycle of sedentary behavior that often contributes to mental fatigue in the first place. By incorporating physical activity into one's daily routine, individuals can establish a healthier balance between work and rest, leading to improved productivity, reduced burnout, and enhanced mental resilience.
