I recommend option (B) Move to a four-day week.

Here are my three strongest reasons:

1. **Increased employee satisfaction and retention**: A four-day week can lead to improved work-life balance, reduced stress, and increased job satisfaction. This, in turn, can result in higher employee retention rates, reduced recruitment costs, and a more stable workforce. A satisfied and engaged workforce is more productive and efficient, which can ultimately benefit the firm's revenue.

2. **Cost savings and reduced overhead**: Implementing a four-day week can lead to significant cost savings, including reduced energy consumption, lower facilities maintenance costs, and decreased transportation expenses. Additionally, the firm may be able to reduce its overhead costs, such as office space and equipment, which can be allocated to other areas of the business.

3. **Competitive advantage and talent attraction**: By adopting a four-day week, the firm can differentiate itself from competitors and attract top talent in a competitive job market. This can be particularly beneficial for a professional services firm, where attracting and retaining skilled professionals is crucial to delivering high-quality services to clients. A four-day week can be a key selling point in attracting and retaining top talent, which can ultimately drive revenue growth.
