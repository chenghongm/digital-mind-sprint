I take position (A) Many small frequent purchases.

Here are my three strongest reasons:

1. **Increased satisfaction and anticipation**: Breaking down a budget into many small frequent purchases can lead to a greater sense of satisfaction and anticipation. When we make small purchases regularly, we get to experience the immediate gratification of acquiring something new, which can release endorphins and create a positive emotional response. In contrast, making a few large purchases may lead to a sense of relief or accomplishment, but it may not provide the same level of ongoing satisfaction.

2. **Improved mental and emotional wellbeing**: Research has shown that the anticipation and excitement of acquiring something new can have a positive impact on mental and emotional wellbeing. This is often referred to as the "hedonic treadmill" effect, where we experience a temporary boost in happiness from a new purchase, but then return to our baseline level of happiness. By making many small frequent purchases, we can create a sense of ongoing excitement and anticipation, which can help to improve our mental and emotional wellbeing.

3. **Increased sense of control and agency**: Making many small frequent purchases can also give us a greater sense of control and agency over our lives. When we have the ability to make small purchases regularly, we feel more in control of our finances and more able to make choices that bring us joy. In contrast, making a few large purchases may lead to a sense of overwhelm or anxiety, particularly if we are unsure about how we will pay for them. By breaking down our budget into many small frequent purchases, we can create a sense of security and stability that can help to improve our overall wellbeing.
