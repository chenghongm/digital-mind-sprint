I take the position that (A) Asynchronous review is better.

Asynchronous review is better for several reasons:

1. **Time zone flexibility**: With team members spread across three time zones, synchronous reviews can be challenging to schedule, especially considering the four-hour daily overlap. Asynchronous reviews allow team members to review code at their convenience, without having to worry about conflicting schedules or time zone differences.

2. **Reduced meeting fatigue**: Synchronous reviews can be lengthy and may lead to meeting fatigue, which can negatively impact team productivity and morale. Asynchronous reviews, on the other hand, allow team members to review code at their own pace, reducing the need for lengthy meetings and minimizing the risk of meeting fatigue.

3. **Improved code review quality**: Asynchronous reviews provide team members with more time to thoroughly review code, reducing the likelihood of rushed or superficial reviews. This allows for more detailed and constructive feedback, which can lead to higher-quality code and better collaboration among team members.
