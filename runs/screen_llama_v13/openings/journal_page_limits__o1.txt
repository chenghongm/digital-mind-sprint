I take position (A) - cap articles at eight pages excluding references.

Here are my three strongest reasons:

1. **Clarity and concision**: Research articles should be clear and concise, conveying the main findings and contributions of the study in a straightforward manner. A page limit encourages authors to focus on the essential aspects of their research, avoiding unnecessary details and tangents. This, in turn, makes it easier for readers to understand and appreciate the work.

2. **Efficient review process**: A page limit helps reviewers to efficiently evaluate submissions, as they can quickly assess the relevance, methodology, and results of the study. Without a page limit, reviewers may struggle to navigate lengthy articles, leading to a more time-consuming and labor-intensive review process. This can delay the publication of accepted articles and hinder the dissemination of research findings.

3. **Consistency and fairness**: Establishing a page limit ensures that all authors are held to the same standards, promoting fairness and consistency in the review process. This also helps to maintain the journal's reputation and credibility, as readers can expect a certain level of quality and brevity from the articles published within. By capping articles at eight pages, the journal can maintain a high level of editorial control and quality, which is essential for a reputable machine learning journal.
