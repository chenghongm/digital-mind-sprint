I take position (A) Require open-access publication.

Here are my three strongest reasons:

1. **Public funding, public benefit**: When research is publicly funded, it is reasonable to expect that the results of that research will be made available to the public. By requiring open-access publication, the benefits of publicly funded research can be shared with everyone, not just those who have access to expensive academic journals. This promotes transparency, accountability, and the advancement of knowledge for the greater good.

2. **Reducing knowledge inequality**: The current system of academic publishing creates a significant barrier to access for researchers in developing countries, small institutions, and individuals without institutional subscriptions. By making research freely available, we can reduce the knowledge gap between those who have access to expensive journals and those who do not. This promotes fairness and equity in the dissemination of knowledge.

3. **Encouraging innovation and collaboration**: Open-access publication can facilitate collaboration and innovation by allowing researchers to build upon existing knowledge without the constraints of paywalls. When research is freely available, it can be more easily cited, referenced, and built upon, leading to a more rapid advancement of knowledge and innovation. This can have significant benefits for society, from improving healthcare to driving economic growth.
