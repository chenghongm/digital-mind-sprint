I take the position that open-plan is worse.

Here are my three strongest reasons:

1. **Distractions and noise**: Open-plan offices often lead to a high level of background noise, which can be distracting and disrupt a developer's focus. Studies have shown that even moderate levels of noise can decrease productivity by up to 66%. In contrast, private offices provide a quieter and more peaceful environment, allowing developers to concentrate on their work.

2. **Collaboration vs. isolation**: While open-plan offices are designed to facilitate collaboration and communication, they can also lead to a sense of isolation and disconnection among developers. Without a dedicated workspace, developers may feel like they're constantly being interrupted or observed, making it difficult to focus on complex tasks. Private offices, on the other hand, provide a sense of ownership and control over one's workspace, allowing developers to work independently and recharge.

3. **Context switching and mental fatigue**: Open-plan offices can lead to a high level of context switching, as developers are constantly being pulled into conversations, meetings, or other distractions. This can cause mental fatigue, decreased productivity, and a higher likelihood of burnout. Private offices, by providing a quiet and distraction-free environment, allow developers to work in a state of flow, minimizing context switching and mental fatigue.

Overall, while open-plan offices may be beneficial for certain types of work or team dynamics, the evidence suggests that private offices are better suited for tasks that require uninterrupted focus and concentration, such as software development.
