I take the position that (B) Asynchronous review is better.

Asynchronous code review is more suitable for an engineering team spread across three time zones with four hours of daily overlap. Here are three reasons why:

1. **Flexibility and Convenience**: Asynchronous review allows team members to review code at their own pace and convenience, without having to adjust their schedules to accommodate a synchronous call. This is particularly beneficial for team members who may have conflicting priorities or time constraints, ensuring that everyone can participate in the review process without feeling rushed or left behind.

2. **Improved Code Quality**: Asynchronous review enables team members to thoroughly review the code, ask questions, and provide feedback without feeling pressured to respond immediately. This leads to higher-quality code reviews, as team members can take the time to carefully evaluate the code, identify potential issues, and suggest improvements. In contrast, synchronous reviews may lead to rushed or superficial reviews, which can compromise code quality.

3. **Reduced Meeting Fatigue**: Synchronous code reviews can be time-consuming and may lead to meeting fatigue, especially for teams with multiple stakeholders or complex codebases. Asynchronous review eliminates the need for lengthy meetings, allowing team members to focus on their work without the distraction of back-to-back calls. This can lead to increased productivity, reduced stress, and a more positive team dynamic.
