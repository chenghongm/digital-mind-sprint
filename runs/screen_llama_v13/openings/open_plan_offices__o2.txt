I take the position that open-plan is worse than private offices when judged by hours of uninterrupted focus time per developer per day.

Here are my three strongest reasons:

1. **Distractions and interruptions**: Open-plan offices often lead to a higher frequency of distractions and interruptions. With colleagues working in close proximity, there's a greater likelihood of conversations, meetings, and impromptu discussions, which can disrupt a developer's focus. In contrast, private offices provide a physical barrier that helps to minimize these distractions, allowing developers to concentrate on their work.

2. **Noise levels and ambient distractions**: Open-plan offices can be noisy, with the constant hum of conversations, keyboard typing, and other background sounds. This ambient noise can be particularly distracting for developers who need to focus on complex tasks that require intense concentration. Private offices, on the other hand, can be designed to be quieter, reducing the likelihood of noise-related distractions.

3. **Context switching and mental fatigue**: When developers are frequently interrupted or distracted, they experience context switching – the mental effort required to refocus on a task after being interrupted. This can lead to mental fatigue, decreased productivity, and a lower quality of work. Private offices can help mitigate this issue by providing a dedicated space for developers to work without interruptions, allowing them to maintain a consistent workflow and reduce mental fatigue.
