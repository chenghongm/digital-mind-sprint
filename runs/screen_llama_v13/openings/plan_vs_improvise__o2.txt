I take the position that a person achieves more by working to a detailed long-term plan.

Firstly, a detailed long-term plan allows for a clear direction and focus, enabling the researcher to allocate their time and resources efficiently. By setting specific goals and milestones, they can prioritize their work, avoid distractions, and make steady progress towards their objectives. This structured approach helps to minimize procrastination and maximize productivity.

Secondly, a long-term plan provides a framework for adaptability and flexibility. While it's essential to be open to new opportunities and changes in the field, a well-planned research career allows for adjustments to be made within the existing framework. This means that the researcher can respond to unexpected opportunities or setbacks while still staying on track with their overall goals.

Thirdly, a detailed long-term plan enables the researcher to make informed decisions about their career trajectory. By setting specific goals and milestones, they can evaluate their progress, identify areas for improvement, and make strategic decisions about their research focus, collaborations, and career development. This helps to ensure that they're making the most of their time and resources, and that their research career is aligned with their long-term aspirations.
