I take position (A) - Require at least 12 weeks of paid parental leave for companies with more than 50 employees.

Here are my three strongest reasons:

1. **Promoting Work-Life Balance and Family Well-being**: Paid parental leave is essential for new parents to bond with their children, recover from childbirth, and establish a stable family routine. Research has shown that paid parental leave leads to improved physical and mental health outcomes for both parents and children. By requiring companies to offer at least 12 weeks of paid parental leave, we can promote a healthier and more balanced work-life environment.

2. **Reducing Turnover and Increasing Productivity**: Companies with paid parental leave policies tend to have lower turnover rates and higher employee satisfaction. When employees feel supported and valued, they are more likely to stay with their employer and contribute to the company's success. Paid parental leave can also lead to increased productivity, as employees are able to return to work refreshed and focused.

3. **Closing the Pay Gap and Supporting Low-Income Families**: In the United States, low-income families are disproportionately affected by the lack of paid parental leave. By requiring companies to offer paid parental leave, we can help close the pay gap and ensure that all families have access to the support they need. This is particularly important for low-income families, who often rely on a single income and may struggle to make ends meet without paid leave.
