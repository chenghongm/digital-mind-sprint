I take position (A) Fully remote.

Here are my three strongest reasons:

1. **Increased flexibility and work-life balance**: Fully remote work allows employees to create their own schedules and work environments, which can lead to improved work-life balance and reduced burnout. This flexibility can also enable employees to take care of personal errands, appointments, or family responsibilities without having to take time off from work. As a result, employees are more likely to be productive and motivated when working remotely.

2. **Reduced office distractions and interruptions**: While some people might think that being in the office is necessary for collaboration and communication, many employees find that office distractions and interruptions can actually decrease productivity. With fully remote work, employees can minimize these distractions and interruptions, allowing them to focus on their tasks and deliver high-quality work.

3. **Broader talent pool and cost savings**: By allowing employees to work remotely, a company can attract and hire the best talent from all over the world, rather than being limited to a specific geographic area. This can lead to a more diverse and skilled workforce, which can drive innovation and success. Additionally, fully remote work can also save the company money on office space, utilities, and other overhead costs, which can be reinvested in employee salaries, training, and other benefits.
