I argue in favor of (B) requiring standardized test scores in university admissions.

Firstly, standardized tests provide a consistent and objective measure of academic ability across different schools and regions, which helps universities compare applicants from diverse backgrounds fairly. Secondly, these tests can serve as a useful predictor of future academic success, allowing institutions to identify students who are likely to thrive in their programs. Lastly, while there are valid concerns about the fairness and validity of standardized tests, they remain a widely accepted and established method for evaluating prospective students, offering a reliable benchmark for admissions decisions.
