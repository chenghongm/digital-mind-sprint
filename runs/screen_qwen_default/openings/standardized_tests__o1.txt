I argue in favor of (A) requiring standardized test scores in university admissions.

Firstly, standardized tests provide a consistent and objective measure of students' academic abilities, which can help universities compare applicants from different backgrounds and schools. This ensures a fair and equitable admissions process where all candidates are evaluated based on similar criteria.

Secondly, these tests often correlate with future academic success, as evidenced by numerous studies showing that SAT and ACT scores are predictive of college performance. By considering these scores, universities can better predict which students are likely to succeed academically, thereby enhancing the overall quality of their student body.

Lastly, standardized tests serve as a gatekeeper for ensuring that students have met certain academic benchmarks before entering higher education. This helps to maintain high standards and prepares students for the rigorous demands of college-level work, which is crucial for their long-term academic and professional development.
