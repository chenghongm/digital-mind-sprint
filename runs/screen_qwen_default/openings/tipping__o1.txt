I argue in favor of (A) replacing tipping with higher fixed wages.

1. **Fairness and Consistency**: Tipping can lead to significant disparities in earnings among servers, depending on their customer base and the tips they receive. By implementing a higher fixed wage, all servers would earn a consistent base salary, regardless of individual performance or luck, leading to more equitable treatment and reducing income inequality within the restaurant industry.

2. **Employee Morale and Retention**: Servers often face high levels of stress and uncertainty due to the variability of their income. A guaranteed higher fixed wage would provide greater job security and reduce financial anxiety, potentially improving overall morale and employee retention rates. This stability could also enhance the quality of service as employees are less likely to be preoccupied with financial concerns.

3. **Simplicity and Transparency**: The current tipping system can be complex and opaque, making it difficult for both employers and employees to understand how much a server should be earning. Implementing a higher fixed wage simplifies payroll processes and ensures that all staff members know exactly what they will be paid, fostering transparency and trust between employers and employees. This clarity can help in building stronger working relationships and a more professional work environment.
