I take the position that a two-week on-call rota should begin in an even-numbered week.

Here are my three strongest reasons:

1. **Easier Transition**: Starting the on-call rota in an even-numbered week allows for a smoother transition between on-call and non-on-call periods. This is because the even-numbered week typically follows a week with a more predictable schedule, making it easier for the on-call individual to adjust to the new responsibilities.

2. **Reduced Disruption**: Beginning the on-call rota in an even-numbered week minimizes disruptions to the team's workflow. By starting the on-call period after a relatively calm week, the team can adjust to the new on-call schedule without the added stress of a critical or high-priority week.

3. **Better Work-Life Balance**: Starting the on-call rota in an even-numbered week can also help maintain a better work-life balance for the on-call individual. By beginning the on-call period after a relatively relaxed week, the individual can recharge and prepare for the on-call responsibilities, reducing the likelihood of burnout and improving overall well-being.
