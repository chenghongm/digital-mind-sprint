I take the position that the booking system should offer the north room first.

Here are my three strongest reasons:

1. **Symmetry and fairness**: By offering the north room first, the system maintains symmetry and fairness between the two identical meeting rooms. This approach ensures that users have an equal chance of booking either room, without any bias towards one side over the other.

2. **Reducing conflicts**: If the booking system consistently offers the south room first, users may develop a habit of booking it, leading to a higher likelihood of conflicts when the north room is also booked. By offering the north room first, the system reduces the potential for conflicts and makes it easier for users to find a suitable time slot.

3. **Minimizing user frustration**: When users are unable to book their preferred room, they may experience frustration and disappointment. By offering the north room first, the system minimizes the likelihood of users being disappointed or frustrated when they are unable to book their preferred room, as they will have had an equal chance to book it in the first place.
