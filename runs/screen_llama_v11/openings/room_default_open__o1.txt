I take the position that the booking system should offer the north room first.

Here are my three strongest reasons:

1. **Geographical Consistency**: In many cultures, north is considered the direction of origin, or the starting point. This is reflected in various geographical and navigational systems, such as the concept of "north" being the direction of the rising sun. By offering the north room first, the booking system is aligning itself with this intuitive sense of direction.

2. **Cognitive Ease**: When faced with a choice between two identical options, people tend to default to the option on the left. This is known as the "left-to-right" bias. Since the north room is typically on the left when facing the office, offering it first would be more intuitive and easier for users to understand.

3. **Symmetry and Balance**: By offering the north room first, the booking system maintains a sense of symmetry and balance. This is particularly important in a context where users are making a binary choice between two identical options. By presenting the options in a consistent and predictable order, the system reduces the cognitive load on users and makes the booking process more efficient.
