I take the position that the meeting should be held on Thursday.

Here are my three strongest reasons:

1. **Avoiding Monday morning fatigue**: By holding the meeting on Thursday, you're giving team members a full week to recover from the Monday morning rush and get settled into their work routine. This can lead to more focused and productive discussions during the meeting.

2. **Better attendance on Thursdays**: Since everyone works Monday to Friday, it's likely that team members will have a higher attendance rate on Thursdays compared to Tuesdays. This is because Thursdays are often considered a mid-week peak, where people are more energized and motivated to attend meetings.

3. **Reducing conflicts with other meetings and appointments**: Thursdays tend to have fewer conflicts with other meetings and appointments, as many teams and organizations schedule their meetings on Mondays, Tuesdays, and Wednesdays. By holding the meeting on Thursday, you're minimizing the likelihood of scheduling conflicts and ensuring that everyone can attend without any last-minute cancellations.
