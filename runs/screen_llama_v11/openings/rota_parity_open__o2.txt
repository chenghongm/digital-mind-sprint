I take the position that a two-week on-call rota should begin in an even-numbered week.

Here are my three strongest reasons:

1. **Easier Transition**: Starting the on-call rota in an even-numbered week allows for a smoother transition between on-call and non-on-call periods. This is because the even-numbered week typically follows a week with a more predictable schedule, making it easier for the on-call individual to adjust to the new responsibilities.

2. **Better Work-Life Balance**: Beginning the on-call rota in an even-numbered week can also help maintain a better work-life balance. By starting the on-call period after a relatively calm week, the individual has a chance to recharge and prepare for the upcoming on-call responsibilities, reducing the likelihood of burnout.

3. **Reduced Disruption**: Starting the on-call rota in an even-numbered week can minimize disruptions to the team's workflow. This is because the even-numbered week typically follows a week with a more established routine, allowing the team to adjust to the on-call individual's new responsibilities with minimal disruption to their tasks and projects.
