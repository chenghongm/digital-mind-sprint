I recommend filling the west block first.

Here are my three strongest reasons:

1. **Natural Traffic Flow**: In most office buildings, the main entrance and reception area are typically located on the west side. As a result, employees and visitors tend to move towards the west side more frequently, which means the west block will likely experience more foot traffic. By placing the most people in the west block, you can minimize disruptions and make the most of the natural flow of people in the building.

2. **Easier Access to Amenities**: In many office buildings, amenities such as the break room, kitchen, and restrooms are often located on the west side. By placing the majority of employees in the west block, they will have easier access to these amenities, reducing the need for long walks and minimizing disruptions to their work.

3. **Reduced Disturbances**: Filling the west block first will also help to reduce disturbances to employees who are already working in the east block. By placing new employees in the west block, you can minimize the number of people moving through the east block, reducing the likelihood of disruptions and distractions to employees who are already settled in their workspaces.
