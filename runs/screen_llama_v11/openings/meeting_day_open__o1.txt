I take the position that a team's weekly planning meeting should be held on Thursday.

Here are my three strongest reasons:

1. **Preparation and Review**: By holding the meeting on Thursday, teams have the entire week to prepare and review their tasks, progress, and goals. This allows them to come to the meeting with a clear understanding of their accomplishments, challenges, and priorities, making the meeting more productive and effective.

2. **Alignment with the Workweek**: Thursday is typically the midpoint of the workweek, allowing teams to assess their progress and adjust their plans accordingly. This timing also helps to ensure that the team is aligned with the workweek's goals and objectives, making it easier to stay on track and meet deadlines.

3. **Reducing Monday Blues**: Starting the week with a planning meeting on Monday can be challenging, especially if team members are still recovering from the weekend or dealing with Monday morning blues. By holding the meeting on Thursday, teams can avoid the post-weekend slump and come to the meeting with a fresh perspective, ready to tackle the rest of the week's challenges.
