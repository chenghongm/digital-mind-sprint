I take the position that a team's weekly planning meeting should be held on Thursday.

Here are my three strongest reasons:

1. **Preparation and Review**: By holding the meeting on Thursday, team members have the entire week to prepare and review their tasks, progress, and goals. This allows them to come to the meeting with a clear understanding of their responsibilities and any challenges they may be facing, making the meeting more productive and effective.

2. **Alignment with the Workweek**: Thursday is typically the midpoint of the workweek, allowing the team to assess their progress and adjust their plans accordingly. This timing also helps to ensure that the team is aligned with the workweek's goals and objectives, making it easier to stay on track and meet deadlines.

3. **Reducing Monday Blues**: Starting the week with a planning meeting on Monday can be overwhelming, especially if team members are still adjusting to their tasks and responsibilities. By holding the meeting on Thursday, the team can avoid the "Monday blues" and come to the meeting with a fresh perspective, ready to tackle the remaining tasks and challenges of the week.
