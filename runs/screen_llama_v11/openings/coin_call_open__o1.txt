I take position (A) Call heads.

Here are my three strongest reasons:

1. **Probability**: In a fair coin toss, the probability of getting heads is 50%, and the probability of getting tails is also 50%. Since both outcomes are equally likely, it's reasonable to assume that one is just as likely to occur as the other. Therefore, calling heads is just as good as calling tails.

2. **Lack of Information**: The person calling the coin toss has no information that would suggest one outcome is more likely than the other. They don't know the coin's history, the tosser's skill level, or any other factor that could influence the outcome. In the absence of any information, it's rational to make a random choice, and calling heads is as random as calling tails.

3. **Symmetry**: The coin toss is a symmetric event, meaning that there's no inherent reason to prefer one outcome over the other. If we were to call tails, we'd be making the same assumption that the other person is making when they call heads. By calling heads, we're not making any assumptions that aren't already implicit in the other person's choice. This symmetry suggests that calling heads is a reasonable and fair choice.
