I take the position that the first shift should begin in an odd-numbered week.

Here are my three strongest reasons:

1. **Even distribution of shifts**: Starting the first shift in an odd-numbered week allows for a more even distribution of shifts across the team over the year. If the first shift begins in an even-numbered week, the team will have to work an extra shift in the first week of the year, which can be challenging and unfair to the team members who are already on call. By starting in an odd-numbered week, the team can maintain a consistent schedule throughout the year.

2. **Avoiding a "double shift" in the first week**: As mentioned earlier, starting the first shift in an even-numbered week would result in a "double shift" in the first week of the year. This can be particularly challenging for the team members who are already on call, as they will have to work an extra shift without any break. By starting in an odd-numbered week, the team can avoid this issue and maintain a consistent schedule.

3. **Simplifying the on-call rota**: Starting the first shift in an odd-numbered week simplifies the on-call rota and makes it easier to manage. It eliminates the need to account for an extra shift in the first week of the year, which can be a complex and time-consuming task. By starting in an odd-numbered week, the team can focus on managing the on-call rota for the rest of the year, without having to worry about the complexities of the first week.
