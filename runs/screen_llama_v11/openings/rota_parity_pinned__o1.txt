I take the position that the first shift should begin in an even-numbered week.

Firstly, starting the on-call rota in an even-numbered week ensures that the team has a full week to adjust to the new schedule before the first shift begins. This allows them to prepare, discuss any concerns, and make necessary arrangements without the pressure of an immediate shift.

Secondly, starting in an even-numbered week helps to distribute the shifts more evenly throughout the year. If the first shift begins in an odd-numbered week, the team will have to work an extra week before the even-numbered weeks start, which can lead to an uneven distribution of shifts. By starting in an even-numbered week, the team can maintain a consistent schedule and avoid any potential imbalances.

Thirdly, starting the on-call rota in an even-numbered week provides a more logical and intuitive structure. It aligns with the typical calendar structure, where weeks are usually numbered starting from 1 (odd) and then 2 (even). This makes it easier for team members to understand and remember the schedule, reducing the likelihood of errors or confusion.
