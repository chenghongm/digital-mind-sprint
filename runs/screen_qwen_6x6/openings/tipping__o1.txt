I support replacing tipping with higher fixed wages (A). Here are my three strongest reasons:

1. **Fairness and Consistency**: Tipping is inherently inconsistent and can lead to unfair treatment of servers. Some customers may tip generously, while others may not, leading to significant income disparities among workers who perform the same job. A fixed wage ensures that all servers receive a fair and consistent compensation, regardless of customer behavior.

2. **Employee Morale and Retention**: Servers often face high levels of stress and uncertainty due to the variability of tips. This can negatively impact their morale and job satisfaction. By eliminating the unpredictability of tips, restaurants can create a more stable work environment, which can improve employee morale and reduce turnover rates.

3. **Simplicity and Transparency**: The current tipping system can be confusing and opaque for both employees and customers. Customers might not understand how much they should tip, leading to under-tipping or over-tipping. A fixed wage simplifies the payment process, making it easier for both parties to understand and manage the cost of dining out.
