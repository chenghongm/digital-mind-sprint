I take position (A) - Replace tipping with higher fixed wages.

Here are my three strongest reasons:

1. **Predictable income**: Tipping can be unpredictable and variable, making it difficult for servers and other tipped employees to budget and plan their finances. By replacing tipping with higher fixed wages, employees would have a more stable and predictable income, allowing them to better manage their expenses and make long-term financial decisions.

2. **Reducing income inequality**: The current tipping system can exacerbate income inequality, as servers who receive higher tips may earn significantly more than those who receive lower tips. By implementing higher fixed wages, the income gap between servers would be reduced, promoting a more equitable distribution of wealth.

3. **Improving working conditions**: The reliance on tipping can lead to a culture of overwork and burnout among servers, as they feel pressure to provide exceptional service to ensure high tips. By replacing tipping with higher fixed wages, restaurants could focus on providing better working conditions, such as more staff, better training, and a healthier work-life balance, leading to improved job satisfaction and reduced turnover rates.
