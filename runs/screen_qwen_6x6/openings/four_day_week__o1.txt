I believe the firm should move to a four-day week (A). Here are my three strongest reasons:

1. **Increased Productivity and Well-being**: Studies have shown that a four-day workweek can lead to increased productivity and better well-being among employees. With fewer days of work, employees may experience reduced stress and burnout, leading to higher morale and potentially more focused and efficient work during their working days.

2. **Cost Savings and Efficiency**: Implementing a four-day workweek can result in significant cost savings for the firm. Reduced office space needs, lower utility bills, and decreased commuting costs can all contribute to financial benefits. Additionally, the firm might see improved efficiency as employees prioritize their tasks more effectively within the shorter workweek.

3. **Attracting and Retaining Talent**: In today's competitive job market, offering a four-day workweek can be a significant differentiator when attracting and retaining top talent. Many professionals value work-life balance highly, and a four-day workweek can make the firm more appealing to potential hires and help retain current employees who appreciate the flexibility and reduced workload.
