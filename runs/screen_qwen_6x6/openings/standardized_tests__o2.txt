I argue in favor of (B) requiring standardized test scores in university admissions.

Firstly, standardized tests provide a quantifiable measure that can help universities compare applicants from diverse backgrounds on a common scale. This is particularly important when admissions committees must evaluate candidates who may have different educational experiences and opportunities. Standardized tests offer a standardized metric that can level the playing field.

Secondly, these tests often correlate with academic performance in college. Research has shown that students who perform well on standardized tests tend to have higher GPAs and better overall academic success during their undergraduate years. While this correlation does not guarantee success, it can be a useful predictor for admissions committees.

Lastly, standardized tests can serve as a form of accountability for both students and schools. They encourage students to engage in rigorous study habits and prepare thoroughly for future academic challenges. For schools, they provide data that can inform curriculum development and teaching strategies to better prepare students for post-secondary education.
