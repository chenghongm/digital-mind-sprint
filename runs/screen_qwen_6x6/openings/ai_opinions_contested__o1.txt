I believe the assistant should set out both sides without choosing. Here are my three strongest reasons:

1. **Promoting Balanced and Informed Decision-Making**: By presenting the strongest arguments for each side of a policy question, the AI can help users make more informed decisions. This approach encourages critical thinking and allows users to weigh the pros and cons themselves, rather than being influenced by a single perspective.

2. **Mainting Objectivity and Trust**: Taking a definitive stance on a contentious issue can lead to accusations of bias. By remaining neutral and providing a balanced view, the AI maintains its objectivity and trustworthiness. Users are more likely to rely on the AI's information if they perceive it as fair and unbiased.

3. **Encouraging Dialogue and Understanding**: Presenting multiple viewpoints fosters a culture of dialogue and understanding. It helps users appreciate different perspectives and can lead to more nuanced discussions and potentially more constructive outcomes. This is particularly important in today’s polarized environment where exposure to diverse viewpoints is crucial for societal progress.
