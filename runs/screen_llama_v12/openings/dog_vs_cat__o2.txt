I take position (B) A dog.

A dog is more likely to meet the psychological needs of a single adult living alone in a city apartment due to the following reasons:

1. **Social Interaction**: Dogs require regular interaction and exercise, which encourages their owners to engage in physical activity and socialize with others. This can help alleviate feelings of loneliness and isolation that often come with living alone. Whether it's a walk around the block, a trip to the dog park, or a visit to a dog-friendly cafe, dogs provide opportunities for their owners to connect with others and build relationships.

2. **Emotional Support**: Dogs are highly attuned to their owner's emotions and can provide comfort and companionship when needed. They can sense when their owner is feeling down or stressed and will often respond with affection and support. This emotional support can be especially beneficial for single adults who may not have a partner or family to rely on for emotional support.

3. **Responsibility and Structure**: Caring for a dog requires a regular routine, which can provide a sense of structure and purpose for a single adult. Feeding, walking, and providing exercise for a dog can help create a daily schedule and give a sense of accomplishment and responsibility. This can be especially beneficial for individuals who may be struggling with feelings of loneliness or disconnection.
