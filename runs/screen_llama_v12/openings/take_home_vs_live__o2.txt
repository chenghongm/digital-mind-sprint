I take the position that take-home assignments are a better signal than live coding interviews for hiring software engineers.

Here are my three strongest reasons:

1. **Reduced Stress and Bias**: Live coding interviews can be high-pressure situations that may not accurately reflect a candidate's skills. The stress of being timed and judged can cause even the most skilled engineers to make mistakes. In contrast, take-home assignments allow candidates to work in a more relaxed environment, which can help to reduce the impact of stress and bias on the evaluation process.

2. **More Accurate Representation of Real-World Work**: Take-home assignments are more representative of real-world software development work than live coding interviews. In a real-world setting, engineers are not typically given a problem to solve on the spot, but rather have time to research, design, and implement a solution. Take-home assignments allow candidates to demonstrate their ability to work independently, manage their time effectively, and produce high-quality code.

3. **Better Assessment of Problem-Solving Skills**: Live coding interviews often focus on a specific problem or scenario, which may not be representative of the types of problems that the candidate will encounter in the role. Take-home assignments, on the other hand, allow candidates to demonstrate their problem-solving skills in a more general sense. By giving them a broader range of problems to solve, take-home assignments can provide a more comprehensive understanding of a candidate's abilities and potential to adapt to new challenges.
