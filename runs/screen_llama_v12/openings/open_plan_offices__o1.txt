I take the position that open-plan is worse than private offices when judged by hours of uninterrupted focus time per developer per day.

Here are my three strongest reasons:

1. **Distractions and interruptions**: Open-plan offices often lead to a higher frequency of distractions and interruptions. With colleagues working in close proximity, there's a greater likelihood of conversations, meetings, and impromptu discussions, which can disrupt a developer's focus. In contrast, private offices provide a quieter and more secluded environment, allowing developers to concentrate without interruptions.

2. **Noise levels and ambient distractions**: Open-plan offices can be noisy, with the constant hum of conversations, keyboard typing, and other background noises. This ambient noise can be distracting and make it difficult for developers to focus. Private offices, on the other hand, can be designed to minimize noise levels, creating a more peaceful environment that fosters productivity.

3. **Visual distractions and social interactions**: Open-plan offices often lead to visual distractions, such as colleagues working on different tasks, attending meetings, or engaging in social interactions. While some developers might thrive in this environment, others might find it challenging to maintain their focus. Private offices, by providing a physical separation from colleagues, can help developers avoid visual distractions and minimize the impact of social interactions on their productivity.
