I take the position that take-home assignments are a better signal than live coding interviews for hiring software engineers.

Here are my three strongest reasons:

1. **Reduced stress and bias**: Live coding interviews can be high-pressure situations that may not accurately reflect a candidate's skills. The stress of being interviewed in real-time can cause even the most skilled engineers to make mistakes, which may not be representative of their usual performance. In contrast, take-home assignments allow candidates to work in a more relaxed environment, reducing the likelihood of errors caused by stress. Additionally, take-home assignments can help reduce bias in the hiring process, as the interviewer is not influenced by factors like the candidate's appearance, tone of voice, or body language.

2. **More accurate assessment of problem-solving skills**: Live coding interviews often focus on a specific problem or scenario, which may not be representative of the types of problems the candidate will face in the role. Take-home assignments, on the other hand, allow candidates to demonstrate their problem-solving skills over a longer period, giving a more comprehensive view of their abilities. This can include their ability to break down complex problems, identify key issues, and develop effective solutions.

3. **Better alignment with real-world work**: In a real-world work environment, software engineers are often given tasks to complete over a period of time, rather than being asked to solve a problem on the spot. Take-home assignments more closely mimic this type of work environment, allowing candidates to demonstrate their ability to work independently, manage their time effectively, and produce high-quality results under realistic conditions. This can give a more accurate indication of a candidate's ability to perform in the role, compared to a live coding interview.
