I take position (B) Many small frequent purchases.

Here are my three strongest reasons:

1. **Increased Utility and Enjoyment**: When we make many small frequent purchases, we are more likely to experience immediate satisfaction and enjoyment from each purchase. This is because small purchases often cater to our immediate needs or desires, providing a sense of instant gratification. In contrast, a few large purchases may not provide the same level of utility and enjoyment, as they may be more focused on long-term goals or investments.

2. **Reduced Opportunity Cost**: By making many small frequent purchases, we are able to allocate our budget to a wider range of goods and services, reducing the opportunity cost of each individual purchase. This means that we are more likely to find products and experiences that bring us joy and satisfaction, rather than being forced to choose between a few large purchases that may not be as fulfilling.

3. **Improved Adaptability and Resilience**: When we make many small frequent purchases, we are more likely to be able to adapt to changing circumstances and unexpected expenses. This is because we have a more flexible budget and can respond quickly to new needs or opportunities. In contrast, a few large purchases may leave us vulnerable to financial shocks or changes in our circumstances, making it more difficult to maintain our long-term wellbeing.
