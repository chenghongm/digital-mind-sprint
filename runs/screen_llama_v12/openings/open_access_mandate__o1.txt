I take position (A) Yes, require open access.

Requiring publicly funded research to publish in open-access venues is essential for the advancement of knowledge and the public good. Here are three strongest reasons why:

1. **Democratization of knowledge**: Open-access publishing ensures that research findings are freely available to everyone, regardless of their institutional affiliation or financial means. This is particularly important for researchers in developing countries or those from underrepresented groups who may not have access to expensive academic journals. By making research publicly available, we can level the playing field and promote a more inclusive and equitable scientific community.

2. **Increased transparency and accountability**: When research is published behind paywalls, it can be difficult for others to verify the findings or replicate the studies. Open-access publishing promotes transparency and accountability by allowing others to scrutinize the research and build upon it. This is especially important in fields like medicine and public health, where timely and accurate information can have significant consequences for public policy and decision-making.

3. **Fostering collaboration and innovation**: Open-access publishing can facilitate collaboration and innovation by making research findings easily accessible to a broader audience. When research is locked behind paywalls, it can be difficult for researchers to build upon existing work or identify potential areas of collaboration. By making research publicly available, we can encourage a more collaborative and interdisciplinary approach to research, leading to new breakthroughs and innovations that benefit society as a whole.
